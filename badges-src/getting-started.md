### Solo / Group Options

- Solo: Activities can be completed individually; reflection replaces collaborative steps.
- Group: Activities encourage negotiation, consensus-building, and shared scheduling.
- Parent/Kid Adaptation: Children can help with journaling and scheduling, using age-appropriate tasks.

## Reflection Prompts

- What motivated you to start this journey?
- Which activity felt easiest? Which was most challenging?
- How can you make your space and schedule work sustainably with what you already have?
- If you’re forming a group, how can you support different abilities and perspectives?
