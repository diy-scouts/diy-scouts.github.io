This badge teaches you how to [skill]. You’ll practice a few simple techniques and complete two small projects. The goal is to help you feel confident, capable, and more connected to your own daily life.

## Step 3: Reflect

_This should be brief and supportive.
2–3 sentences + optional prompts._

**Template text:**

After you finish your tasks, take a moment to reflect. Reflection helps you notice what you learned and how your new skill fits into your life.

Choose one prompt to answer:

- What felt easiest?
- What surprised you?
- How might you use this skill again?
- What would you like to learn next?
