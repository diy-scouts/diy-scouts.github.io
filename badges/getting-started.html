🌱 Getting Started Badge

Type: Free – Life Skills

Purpose: Establish a foundation for participation — forming a group or going solo, setting norms, scheduling, and creating reflection practices.

Badge Overview

Welcome to your adventure! This badge helps you set up your journey with clarity, intention, and flexibility. Whether you’re starting solo or with friends, you’ll create the structures and habits that let you explore DIY Scouting.

Activities

Pick at least 4 of the 7 following activities.

For each activity, pick a difficulty level that sounds challenging but doable. If none of the options meet your needs, come up with your own way to fulfill the spirit of the activity:

Decide whether you’ll participate solo, form a new group, or join an existing one.

  • Light: Identify 1 person or go solo
  • Moderate: Invite 2–3 peers
  • Challenge: Form a group of 4–6 with agreed goals

Establish basic guidelines: meeting cadence, communication, values, accessibility, and participation expectations.

  • Light: List 3 basic norms
  • Moderate: Draft full group agreement
  • Challenge: Facilitate group discussion to finalize norms collaboratively

Set a rhythm for meetings, reflection, and badge activities.

  • Light: Pick one time per week
  • Moderate: Create 2–3 sessions per month
  • Challenge: Build a flexible quarterly schedule for long-term planning

Track progress, thoughts, and reflections. Encourage sketching, notes, photos, or digital entries.

  • Light: One-page first entry
  • Moderate: Weekly entries for a month
  • Challenge: entries for a month Integrate prompts for Light/Moderate/Challenge activities from future badges

Identify materials, tools, or skills you already have. Focus on second-hand, shared, or upcycled resources.

  • Light: List 5 items
  • Moderate: List 10+ resources for sharing
  • Challenge: Create a mini “community toolbox” with group contributions

Prepare your sticker page, spreadsheet, or digital tracker to log badge completion.

  • Light: Print out a Badge page or use website to track
  • Moderate: Digital tracker with notes
  • Challenge: Add reflection prompts and progress markers for each activity

Share your intentions with a broader community, such as a Discord channel, social media hashtag, or peer network.

  • Light: Join one community
  • Moderate: Post first reflection
  • Challenge: Facilitate one small discussion or micro-challenge

Solo / Group Options

  • Solo: Activities can be completed individually; reflection replaces collaborative steps.
  • Group: Activities encourage negotiation, consensus-building, and shared scheduling.
  • Parent/Kid Adaptation: Children can help with journaling and scheduling, using age-appropriate tasks.

Reflection Prompts

  1. What motivated you to start this journey?

  2. Which activity felt easiest? Which was most challenging?

  3. How can you make your space and schedule work sustainably with what you already have?

  4. If you’re forming a group, how can you support different abilities and perspectives?
